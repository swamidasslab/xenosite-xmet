title: XMET
headline: A shared vocabulary for xenobiotic metabolism
summary: Stable, defined names for how drugs and other foreign compounds are transformed in the body.
---

When a drug or other foreign compound is metabolized, chemists describe what happened in many different ways: *N-demethylation*, *oxidative N-dealkylation*, *loss of CH₂*, *−14 Da*. XMET gives each of these ideas one stable, defined name, so that datasets, prediction tools, and people can agree on what a metabolic reaction is.

XMET currently defines **{{stats.concepts}} concepts**, organized into a single browsable hierarchy. Each concept has a preferred label, a plain-language definition, common synonyms, and a permanent identifier that resolves to its page here.

XMET is under active curation. Identifiers are stable and never reused; labels, definitions, and placement may still change.
